## 1. The fault pause waited only 5 s (Mega)

- **Problem:** a dose paused too soon with the "check clogging" fault (EMPTY: less than
  1 g came out while the auger ran).
- **Change:** `EMPTY_WINDOW_MS` from 5 s to **15 s**, in `AgriDose_Mega_Link` and
  `main_checker`. The 4-minute TIMEOUT per hopper is unchanged.
- Top-ups (TRIM) also wait 15 s now before giving up.

## 2. "Continue" sometimes restarted from 0 g (Mega)

- **Cause:** the Mega counts grams from the hopper's drop in weight. At a fault it
  weighed the jug again and used that as the new starting point. A hand pushing on the
  jug (tapping it to clear a clog) made the jug read heavier. The count then went to 0
  or below, and after `#` the hopper got its whole share again.
- **Shown in the simulator:** with a 120 g push, hopper A gave **269.7 g instead of 150 g**.
  After the fix it gives **149.2 g**.
- **Fix:** each hopper keeps a running record of the grams already out, and that number
  never goes down during a dose. A re-weigh more than 10 g below it is ignored, and
  Serial Monitor prints `jug touched? keeping those`.
- **Not covered:** a hand on the jug at the exact moment `#` is pressed, which is when the
  new start weight is taken. Keep hands off when the LCD says `HANDS CLEAR?`.

## 4. Stock check before a dose (ESP32)

- **Rule:** a hopper is short when it holds less than its share of the dose plus
  **20 g spare** (`STOCK_SPARE_G` in `config.h`).
- **Keypad:** after typing the mass, a short hopper shows `LOW COMPOST` /
  `NEED 650 HAS 410`, then `REFILL HOPPER OR` / `#=GO ON *=BACK`.
  - Refill it: the screen switches to CONFIRM by itself.
  - `#`: start anyway. Running out only pauses the dose.
  - `*`: type a smaller mass.
- **Dashboard:** the Machine tab has a new panel, **Enough stock for**. It shows the
  biggest dose of each recipe the hoppers can give, and which hopper to refill.
- **Tests:** `tests/test_stock.cpp` 18/18, dashboard test 101/101. The laptop mock gives
  the same answers as the firmware on 2000 random stocks.

## 6. Low-hopper alert on the bell (dashboard)

- **Rule:** a hopper under **200 g** (its last `HW` weight) puts the red dot on the bell.
  The bell lists each one: `Hopper B (compost) is low: 150 g left, under 200 g. Refill it
  before the next dose.` The limit is `LOW_HOPPER_G` in `www/app.js`.
- Worked out on the phone; no firmware logic changed for it (the page is still in the
  ESP32's flash, so it comes with the ESP32 flash). It is only a warning: whether a dose
  fits is still the stock check (section 4).
- **Tests:** dashboard test 104/104 (full hoppers give no alert; 150 g and below zero
  each give one; exactly 200 g gives none; the red dot and the bell's list).

## 7. Drain a hopper (keypad D, both boards)

- **Keypad:** HOME now reads `AGRIDOSE D=DRAIN` / `#=DOSE  0=ZERO`. `D` opens
  `DRAIN  A=HULL` / `B=COMP C=ASH`. Pick a hopper: `COMP HAS 4123g` / `#=DRAIN *=BACK`.
  `#` starts it. To drain compost: `D` `B` `#`, and `*` to stop.
- `D` in any other menu still goes home, as before. The short names HULL and ASH were
  Claude's choice (only `B=COMP` was given).
- **Mega:** runs that one auger at full speed. LCD `DRAINING COMP` / `OUT 1234g *=STOP`.
  It stops on `*`, by itself after 15 s with no flow (`NO FLOW: EMPTY?`), on a dead load
  cell (`DRAIN: NO SENSOR`) and after 10 minutes (`DRAIN TIME UP`). Then it shows
  `COMP 1234g OUT` for 3 s.
- **Not a dose:** no log row, the learned stop-early grams don't change, and nothing is
  saved, so after a power cut the auger stays off. A dose, zeroing or resume is refused
  while a drain runs, and a drain is refused during a dose.
- **Dashboard:** Machine Status says **Draining**.
- **Tests:** Mega simulator 80/80 (14 new drain checks: empties and stops, `*`, refusals,
  jam, dead load cell, the 10-minute limit, power cut, a dose straight after a drain),
  `test_agrilink.cpp` all pass. Both sketches compile: ESP32 91 % (also with
  `FAKE_MEGA`, where `DB#` drains hopper B), Mega 12 % flash, 48 % RAM.
- **Not tested yet:** the ESP32's drain screens and keys were only compiled. Nothing has
  run on a real board. Try it first with `FAKE_MEGA 1` or on the bench (README step 10).
- **Choices made by Claude, to confirm:** the screen texts, full speed, the 15 s no-flow
  stop, the 10-minute limit, and that the material leaves through the dose outlet.
